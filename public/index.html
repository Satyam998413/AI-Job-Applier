02 · Evolution Strategy

From local prototype to commercial gateway

The Motor ESP32 is upgraded into a Smart Cloud Gateway built around three guarantees.

Local persistent buffering (fail-safe storage)

Telemetry is written directly to the Motor ESP32's internal non-volatile flash file system. If the home internet or router drops, reports are buffered in local hardware storage so no data is lost.

Asynchronous cloud sync (reporting engine)

When connectivity returns, the gateway packages accumulated logs and batches them to Firebase — providing historical streams to the web panel and updating the mobile app in under 400 ms.

Single-use industrial battery system

Node 1 sits on open roofs with no power sockets. Li-SOCl₂ chemistry + a supercapacitor for pulse loads, plus extreme deep-sleep rules (waking only 4× a day), give 2–3 years with no cords or solar.

05 · Product Models

Model A vs Model B

Node 1 (Tank Unit) is identical in both. Only Node 2's control, valve and cleaning strategy differ.

🌾 Model A — Frugal Retrofit

Agricultural / Village

  • Motor (Pulse method): two 5 V relays in parallel inside the existing starter box; a 1 s pulse "presses" the Green/Red buttons — no heavy current.
  • Valve (Clamp-on): MG996R servo clamps over the manual PVC ball-valve handle, rotating it 90° — no pipe cutting.
  • Cleaning (Bottom siphon flush): perforated floor pipe; servo opens valve 45 s, water weight siphons silt out — no pump power, tank not emptied.

System total ₹5,050

🏙️ Model B — Premium Advanced Grid

Urban Residential

  • Motor (Direct switching): bypasses legacy panels, runs pump AC mains through a 30 A power relay.
  • Valve (Inline actuator): commercial 12 V motorized inline valve built into the pipe.
  • Cleaning (Closed filter loop): 100 % zero-waste — a 12 V circulation pump runs muddy water through an external sand/mesh/charcoal filter back to the top.

System total ₹6,230
